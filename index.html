Before the day arrives, a few things you should know

Alina, I keep checking the calendar, hoping it will move faster if I stare hard enough. It won't. So instead, I made you this small page to hold the waiting.

You have a way of making ordinary days feel special — a plain Tuesday, a boring lecture, a short phone call between classes. I don't think you even notice you're doing it. I do.

So this countdown isn't really about a date. It's counting down to a day that is fully, simply yours — and I plan to spend all of it reminding you why.

Until then: drink some water, try to sleep on time, and know that somewhere, someone is genuinely excited for your birthday.
