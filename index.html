Alina, jaan — I keep looking at the calendar like it's going to move faster if I stare hard enough. It won't. So instead I'm building you this silly, sweet little page to hold the waiting.

You have this way of making ordinary days feel like they mattered — a random Tuesday, a boring lecture, a five-minute phone call between classes. I don't think you even notice you're doing it. I do.

So this countdown isn't really counting down to a date. It's counting down to a day that's entirely, unapologetically about you — and I intend to spend every minute of it reminding you why.

Until then: drink water, sleep on time (I know, I know), and know that somewhere, someone is genuinely this excited about your birthday.
